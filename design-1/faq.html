FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your free trial or starting treatment.

Do I need a GP referral to book?

No referral is needed for private physiotherapy. You can book directly online. If you're using health insurance, check your policy — some insurers require a GP referral before they'll cover treatment.

What happens during the free trial consultation?

Your 30-minute free session includes a structured consultation where your coach asks pre-written clinical questions covering your medical history, current symptoms, lifestyle, and goals. This ensures nothing important is missed. You'll receive initial recommendations and an overview of your options (sessions, courses, or books).

Is the free trial really free?

Yes — completely free with no payment details required. There is no obligation to book further sessions or purchase anything after your trial.

How long are follow-up sessions?

Standard follow-up sessions are 45 minutes (£75). Extended 60-minute sessions are available for complex cases (£95). We also offer a block of 6 sessions for £400 (saving £50).

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes — we accept Bupa, AXA Health, Vitality, WPA, and most major insurers. We provide invoices for self-pay patients to claim back through their insurer. Contact us if you're unsure about your cover.

Can I have online/video sessions?

Yes. Your free trial can be conducted via video call. Follow-up sessions are available both in-person at our London clinic and online via secure video.

What should I wear to my appointment?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows access to the area being treated. For lower body issues, shorts are ideal. For upper body, a vest or t-shirt works well.

How many sessions will I need?

This varies by condition. Your coach will give you an estimated timeline during your free trial. Simple issues may resolve in 2–4 sessions; complex or chronic conditions may need 8–12+ sessions.

What's the difference between sessions, courses, and books?

Sessions are live one-on-one appointments with your physiotherapist. Courses are self-paced online programmes with video lessons and exercises. Books are written guides for home reference. Your coach will recommend the best option after your free trial.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged at 50% of the session fee.
