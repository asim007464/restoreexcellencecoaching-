FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about booking, treatment, and payment.

Do I need a GP referral?

No — book directly for private physio. Check your insurance policy if using cover.

What happens in the free trial?

A 30-minute structured consultation with pre-written assessment questions covering history, symptoms, and goals. You'll receive recommendations and an overview of sessions, courses, and books.

Is it really free?

Yes — no payment details required. Zero obligation to continue.

Session length and cost?

45-minute sessions are £75. Extended 60-minute sessions £95. Block of 6 sessions £400.

Insurance accepted?

Bupa, AXA, Vitality, WPA, and most major insurers. Invoices provided for claims.

Online sessions available?

Yes — free trial and follow-ups available via secure video call.

What to wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing. Shorts for lower body; vest/t-shirt for upper body.

Sessions vs courses vs books?

Sessions are live 1-on-1. Courses are self-paced online. Books are home reference guides. Your coach recommends the best fit after your trial.

Cancellation policy?

24 hours notice required. Late cancellations charged at 50%.
