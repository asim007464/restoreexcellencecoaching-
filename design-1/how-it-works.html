How It Works

From your free trial to ongoing care — here's exactly what to expect at every stage.

1

Book Your Free Trial Class

Choose a convenient time slot and fill in a brief intake form. Your first consultation is completely free — no payment details required.

2

Meet Your Coach (Video or In-Person)

During your session, your physiotherapist will guide you through a structured consultation. Pre-written assessment questions ensure we cover your medical history, current symptoms, lifestyle, and recovery goals thoroughly.

3

Receive Your Personalised Plan

Based on the discussion, your coach will outline recommended next steps — whether that's targeted exercises, further sessions, or self-guided resources.

4

Choose How to Continue

After your free trial, pick the path that suits you: book follow-up sessions on our schedule, enrol in a structured course, or purchase one of our expert recovery books.
